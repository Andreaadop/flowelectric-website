Regina's Leaders in Aluminum Wiring Remediation

Since 1965, aluminum branch wiring was installed in thousands of Regina homes. Today, many homeowners face panic from insurance companies or home inspectors. At Flow Electric, we don't believe in scare tactics. We believe in science, the Canadian Electrical Code, and proven remediation.

Aluminum wiring is not a death sentence for your home or your insurance policy. It is a manageable system that requires specialized expertise, proper maintenance, and professional documentation. We specialize in the mid-century electrical systems found throughout Albert Park, Normanview, Argyle Park, and Glencairn.

The Real Risks — And How We Address Them

Aluminum expands and contracts more than copper. Over time, connections can loosen, resistance builds, and heat develops. The risks come down to:

  • Loose terminations — we torque every connection to manufacturer specs
  • Improper dissimilar-metal splices — we use AlumiConn connectors, the only UL-listed method
  • Outdated devices not rated for aluminum — we replace and pigtail correctly
  • Undersized conductors for modern loads — we verify before adding EV chargers, hot tubs, or AC

We don't just pigtail wires. We use AlumiConn connectors and approved AL-CU rated marrettes — the methods insurance inspectors want to see.

The Insurance Solution

Buying a home in Whitmore Park or Lakeview? If your broker is asking for an Aluminum Wiring Electrical Report, we can help. We provide the specific documentation required by SGI, CAA, and Wawanesa to ensure your home insurance is approved without delay.

Our full-scale inspection reports include: panel condition, termination inspection, service size confirmation, bonding verification, documentation of pigtailing, and thermal imaging. We complete insurer-specific forms when required, speeding up approvals and reducing underwriting friction.

The Flow Method

  • Pigtail Standard — approved AL-CU marrettes (63P style and AlumiConn) to stabilize terminations
  • Torque Verification — every connection set to manufacturer specs, not "hand tight"
  • Antioxidant Protection — a layer of corrosion defense where applicable
  • Full Documentation — a digital report you can email directly to your broker
