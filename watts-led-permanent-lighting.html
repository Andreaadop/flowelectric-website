Watts LED Permanent
Lighting Regina

The most advanced permanent LED lighting system available — certified Watts installers in Regina. Built for Saskatchewan winters, controlled from your phone.

Not Your Average LED Lights

At Flow Electric, we exclusively trust and install Watts LED Lights — a brand known for offering the most advanced and reliable LED lighting solutions on the market. As certified Watts installers, we ensure your lighting system is installed with precision and care, enhancing both the beauty and safety of your property.

In addition to standard RGB LEDs, Watts lights are equipped with a warm white LED for stunning architectural accent throughout the year. Each pixel light features 12 LED segments, allowing up to 16.7 million different colours including pure white. Vivid, bright, with even saturation — no hot spots or dim zones.

Built for Regina's Climate

Watts LED Lights are engineered to withstand the toughest conditions. Whether frozen in ice or submerged in water, Watts lights continue to work — even at -50°C. Built and designed in Calgary, they're purpose-made for prairie winters.

  • IP68 water and dust resistance rating — the highest available
  • Operates in temperatures as low as -50°C
  • Rated for 50,000 hours of use
  • Full CSA certification and built-in surge protection
  • 5-year parts warranty on all lights
  • Low voltage 12V DC — safe and energy efficient (0.72W per light at full brightness)

Installation & Control

Watts lights are installed using a custom-built track system that blends into your roofline — nearly invisible during the day, stunning after dark. The user-friendly app gives you access to 16+ million colours and patterns, cloud-based control from anywhere, voice commands, and scheduling.

Where Can Watts LED Lights Be Installed?

  • Front and rear rooflines of your home
  • The entire roofing structure
  • Fences, gazebos, pergolas, and hot tub areas
  • Commercial storefronts and building facades

Common Questions

  • How long does installation take? — Most 2-storey homes: 1 day. Bungalows: half-day. Large homes may require 2+ days.
  • Can I modify the system myself? — No. Unauthorized modifications void all warranties. Call us for any changes.
  • Do they need maintenance? — No regular maintenance required. The system is built to run for years unattended.
  • Commercial installations? — Absolutely. We have several commercial clients and can design custom systems for any application.
