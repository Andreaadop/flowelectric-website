About Us

Installing Power
Where You Need It.

A family-built electrical company serving Regina since day one — Red Seal certified, fully insured, and committed to getting it right.

A company of families, building together.

Flow Electric is a tight, premium electrical firm dedicated to solving real problems with quality workmanship and clean execution. Our team brings a wealth of experience to both residential and commercial electrical projects, capable of handling new constructions, renovations, or specific electrical tasks with efficiency and expertise.

We believe in doing the job right the first time — using certified materials, proper documentation, and journeyman-supervised work on every project. That commitment to craft is what sets us apart from every other electrician in Regina.

Whether it's a service upgrade, an EV charger, a panel problem, or a full commercial build, Flow Electric shows up prepared, communicates clearly, and leaves the site clean.
