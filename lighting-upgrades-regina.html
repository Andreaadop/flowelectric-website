Lighting Upgrades
Regina

From recessed LED retrofits to full rewires — we design and install lighting that improves function, efficiency, and atmosphere in your home.

Transform Your Space With Better Light

Lighting upgrades go beyond simple bulb replacements — they're about transforming your space into a more energy-efficient, visually appealing, and functional environment. LED technology brightens your home while lowering power consumption, and our range of lighting solutions helps you craft the perfect atmosphere for every room.

Our Lighting Services

  • LED Upgrades — shift to LED lighting for superior efficiency, reliability, and longevity
  • Pot Lights / Recessed Lighting — sleek, modern pot lights that integrate seamlessly into any room
  • Fixture Updates — modernize your space with updated fixtures that refresh style and illumination
  • Kitchen & Bathroom Lighting — targeted upgrades with the right colour temperature for each space
  • Outdoor Lighting — boost exterior appeal with strategic lighting that enhances safety and architecture
  • Commercial Specialty Lighting — optimized for efficiency and control in commercial settings

Planning Your Upgrade

When considering a lighting upgrade, we assess your space's natural light, your task lighting needs, and your desired ambiance before recommending a solution. We also consider advanced controls like dimmers and smart switches for greater energy savings and comfort.

What You Should Know

  • Installing a ceiling light typically runs $65–$175 for labour, depending on the fixture
  • Smart home lighting may require specific wiring — we assess before we quote
  • LED retrofits dramatically reduce power consumption vs. older fluorescent or incandescent systems
  • Outdoor lighting requires weatherproofing and proper grounding — never a DIY job

We source quality fixtures and LED components from trusted suppliers and complete all work to CEC standards with a clean job site left behind.
