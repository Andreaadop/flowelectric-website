Expert EV Charging Station Installation in Regina

Having a personal EV charging station offers unparalleled convenience — charge your vehicle overnight and start each day with a full battery. Home charging stations provide faster speeds than public chargers and are more cost-effective over time. With Flow Electric, you get a professional installation that ensures safety, efficiency, and optimal performance.

We assess your vehicle's requirements, your home's electrical system, and your charging habits to recommend the best solution. Our goal is seamless integration — the charger fits your home and your needs from day one.

What's Included in an EV Charger Installation

  • Site assessment — garage layout, panel location, run distance
  • Panel capacity check — ensure your service can handle the added load
  • Dedicated 240V, 40–60A circuit installation
  • Level 2 EVSE mounting and wiring (we work with all major charger brands)
  • TSASK permit and electrical inspection
  • Full test and commissioning

Do You Need a Panel Upgrade First?

An EV charger adds more electrical demand than a whole kitchen. Many older Regina homes — especially those built before 1980 — have 60A or 100A services that are already running near capacity. Before we run an EV circuit, we perform a CEC-based load calculation.

If your panel needs an upgrade, we can combine both projects into a single mobilization — saving you time and cost. We're experienced at coordinating with SaskPower for service upgrades that include EV charging as the end goal.

Why Not Use the 120V Outlet in Your Garage?

Level 1 charging (a standard outlet) adds about 8km of range per hour. A Level 2 charger adds 30–50km per hour. For daily driving in Regina, Level 1 is simply too slow. If you're plugging in at night and need a full charge by morning, Level 2 is the right choice.

Compatible with All EV Models

We install charging infrastructure that works with Tesla, Ford, GM, Hyundai, Volkswagen, Rivian, and every other EV on the market. The J1772 connector standard or Tesla's NACS adapter makes any Level 2 charger compatible with any vehicle sold in Canada.
