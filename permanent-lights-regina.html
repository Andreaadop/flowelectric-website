Permanent Lighting
Regina

Year-round architectural LED lighting installed in your eaves — no clips, no ladders, no seasonal hassle. Control colours and schedules from your phone.

No More Climbing Ladders Every December

Permanent eave-mounted LED lighting is installed once and enjoyed all year. Control colours, patterns, and schedules from your phone. Christmas lights, Halloween effects, Canada Day red and white — all on a schedule you set once and forget. No more tangled strings, no more falling off ladders, no more staple guns in the cold.

What We Install

We are certified installers of professional-grade permanent lighting systems including Watts LED — the most advanced system available for Canadian climates. These lights are engineered to withstand temperatures as low as -50°C and come with an IP68 water resistance rating.

  • Built into your roofline using a custom track system — nearly invisible during the day
  • 16+ million colour options with per-pixel control via a simple app
  • Low voltage 12V DC — safe and energy efficient
  • Rated for 50,000 hours — effectively a lifetime of use
  • Full CSA certification and built-in surge protection
  • 5-year parts warranty on all Watts LED components

Professional Installation — Done Right Once

Most 2-storey homes can be completed in a single day. Bungalows are typically a half-day. Installation includes the track system, all LED runs, the low-voltage controller, and wiring to your electrical panel. We also ensure the installation complies with local bylaws and building regulations.

Where Can Lights Be Installed?

  • Front and rear rooflines
  • Entire roofing structure
  • Fences, gazebos, pergolas, and hot tub areas
  • Commercial storefronts and building facades

Any modifications to the system must be completed by an authorized technician — unauthorized changes void all warranties. That's why Flow Electric is your single point of contact from initial install through warranty service.
