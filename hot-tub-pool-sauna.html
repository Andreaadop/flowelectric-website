Safe Backyard Electrical for Hot Tubs, Pools & Saunas

Adding a hot tub or sauna to your home is not just an upgrade — it's an investment in your health and well-being. These installations offer a private retreat where you can unwind, relieve stress, and soothe muscles. Perfect for harsh Regina winters, they provide a warm sanctuary right in your backyard.

Backyard amenities require dedicated circuits, GFCI protection, and proper bonding. Water and electricity are a dangerous combination — our certified journeyman electricians ensure everything is safe, compliant, and ready for your family to enjoy.

What's Involved in Hot Tub & Pool Wiring

  • Dedicated 240V circuit from your electrical panel
  • GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) protection — required by code
  • Proper bonding of the tub, pump, and surrounding metallic components
  • Load calculation to confirm your panel can handle the added demand
  • TSASK permit pulled and inspection arranged
  • Weatherproof disconnect switch installed within sight of the tub

1-Day Installation — Start Relaxing Sooner

We specialize in fast, efficient installations that let you enjoy your new backyard oasis without a lengthy wait. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped with the latest tools to ensure your hot tub or sauna is installed correctly and efficiently — often in a single day.

Panel Capacity Check

Hot tubs and pools draw significant power — typically 50–60 amps for a hot tub, more for a pool pump. Before we run a circuit, we perform a full load calculation to confirm your panel has the capacity. If it doesn't, we can upgrade the panel at the same time, saving you a second mobilization cost.

Installation for Pools

Inground and above-ground pools require bonding grids, properly rated pumps, and underwater lighting circuits. We design and install the complete electrical system to code so you can open your pool with confidence.
