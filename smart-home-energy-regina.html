Smart Home
Electrical Regina

We wire the infrastructure behind your smart home — dedicated circuits for automation hubs, thermostats, lighting control, security, and energy monitoring. Done right, from the panel out.

Smart Homes Need Smart Electrical Infrastructure

Smart home technology is only as good as the electrical infrastructure behind it. Controlling your home's lighting, temperature, security, and entertainment systems is only possible when your wiring was done correctly. Flow Electric specializes in building the electrical foundation your smart home needs to operate seamlessly.

Whether you're outfitting a new build or upgrading an existing Regina home, we assess your system requirements and install dedicated circuits, proper neutral wiring, and clean panel organization that supports every device in your automation ecosystem.

What We Wire for Smart Homes

  • Dedicated circuits for automation hubs and network equipment
  • Smart thermostat wiring — including C-wire (common wire) additions where missing
  • Lighting control circuits with neutral wires at switches for smart dimmers
  • Whole-home energy monitoring systems at the panel
  • Security system power and camera circuits
  • Home theatre and AV equipment dedicated circuits

The Benefits of Proper Smart Home Wiring

Smart devices fail and frustrate when the electrical infrastructure is wrong. Dimmers that flicker, thermostats that don't respond, hubs that reboot constantly — these are wiring problems, not app problems. We eliminate those issues before they start.

  • Stable, dedicated power to all automation hubs
  • No flickering or dropout on smart lighting circuits
  • Real-time energy data from whole-home monitoring
  • Clean panel organization that makes future upgrades easy

Eaton AbleEdge Smart Panels

When we install or upgrade your electrical panel, we recommend Eaton BR-style panels with AbleEdge™ Smart Breakers compatibility. This gives you real-time energy monitoring per circuit — so you can see exactly what your EV charger, AC, or smart appliances cost you month to month. It's smart home monitoring built into the panel itself.

Not Sure What You Need?

Tell us what devices you want to control — lighting, heating, security, EV, entertainment — and we'll design the electrical infrastructure to support it. We work alongside your smart home installer or directly with you if you're handling the device setup yourself.
