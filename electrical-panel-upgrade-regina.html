Electrical Panel
Upgrades Regina

Moving your home from 1950s infrastructure to 2026 capacity. 100A and 200A upgrades for insurance compliance, EV chargers, and modern living.

Regina's Authority in Panel & Service Changes

In Regina, your electrical panel doesn't care about brand as much as it cares about amperage and age. If you're running a 60A or 70A service, your home insurance policy is at risk. We specialize in upgrading these aging systems to modern 100A or 200A standards — keeping your coverage active and your home safe.

Before you add an EV charger, hot tub, or AC, we perform a CEC-based load calculation. We work directly with TSASK to ensure your home has the legally required capacity to handle your lifestyle without risking a fire or a refusal to energize from the utility.

The Flow Process: 8–10 Hours to Total Peace of Mind

A panel change isn't a 2-hour job if it's done right. We tell our clients to expect an 8–10 hour power outage. We start early, coordinate with SaskPower, and have your lights back on before dinner.

  • Full load calculation under TSASK requirements
  • Coordination with the SaskPower Regina District office
  • Meter base replacement (older bases don't meet today's code)
  • Grounding update to 2024 CEC standards
  • TSASK permit pulled and inspection arranged — we handle it all

Smart Power for Smart Homes

We exclusively install Eaton BR-style panels, compatible with AbleEdge™ Smart Breakers — allowing real-time energy monitoring so you can see exactly what your EV charger or AC costs you every month.

Service vs. Panel — What's the Difference?

  • The Service — the line from SaskPower's tie point to your meter
  • The Meter — older Regina meters often need replacement to meet current code
  • The Panel — the breaker box inside your home that distributes power

Most jobs require upgrading all three. We source our Eaton components from local suppliers like Westburne to keep lead times short.

Common Questions

  • Do I need a larger service for an EV? — Usually yes. An EV charger adds more demand than a whole kitchen.
  • Will insurance require an upgrade? — Many insurers now flag 60A/70A services as uninsurable.
  • Cost? — A professional 100A or 200A upgrade typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000+ depending on complexity.
